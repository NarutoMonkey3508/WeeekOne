Questions

Straight answers

Do I need any tech skills?

No. I do all the setup. You send me your details and photos, then tell me what you think of the demo.

Who owns the website?

You do. Your domain is registered in your name, and the site, logo and menu are yours to keep, even if you stop working with me.

Are there any extra costs?

Only your domain name, about $20 a year. The care plan is optional at $25 a month.

Every package includes 2 rounds of changes. More rounds cost extra, and I always tell you the price first.

How long does it take?

I reply within 24 hours and send your free demo within 3 days. Once you approve it, you're live in about a week.

I already have a website. Can you help?

Yes. I can rebuild an outdated site, or just fix your Google listing and Instagram so they match. Send me the link and I'll take a look.

When and how do I pay?

Only after you've seen and approved your demo. A parent emails you a PayPal invoice, and you can pay it by card or PayPal.

Local clients can also pay by Interac e-Transfer. The care plan is a monthly invoice.

What exactly is included?

Before I start, I write down exactly what's in your package and you agree to it. No surprises on either side.

Online stores, apps, logins and payment systems aren't included. If you need one, ask and I'll quote it separately or tell you honestly if I can't help.

Is the demo really free?

Yes. No card and no commitment. If it's not right for you, you don't pay anything.

Who runs WeeekOne?

WeeekOne is a student-run studio. I build every site myself.

A parent handles invoices and agreements, so the paperwork is always done properly.
